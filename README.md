# Akazi.com - Find Work. Hire Talent. Grow.

**Akazi.com** is an online platform based in Rwanda designed to bridge the gap between employment seekers and providers. 
It serves as a comprehensive hub linking job seekers with employers, assisting students and professionals in securing internships, providing corporate recruitment consultancy,
and connecting authors with readers.

---

## 🚀 Vision & Mission

*   **Vision:** To be the number one trusted platform in Africa where everyone can find a job and every company can find the right worker.
*   **Mission:** To connect job seekers and employers quickly and fairly, support young people with internships, and provide writers a place to connect with readers.

---

## ✨ Core Values

*   **Trust:** Only real and verified jobs are published.
*   **Equal Chance:** Creating an environment where everyone has the right to get a job.
*   **Simplicity:** User-friendly website navigation designed for everyone.
*   **Support:** Offering tailored help and advice to help job seekers succeed.
*   **Innovation:** Utilizing technology to streamline and accelerate the job search process.

---

## 🛠️ Our Services

*   **Job Seekers:** Online job advertisements, career advice, and professional CV writing support.
*   **Employers:** Direct recruitment, corporate consultancy, and cost-effective business/company advertisements.
*   **Students & Professionals:** Academic and professional internship placement opportunities.
*   **Writers:** A digital marketplace to seamlessly connect book writers directly with buyers and readers.

---

## 🗺️ Website Structure & Menu Navigation

The platform features a clean, accessible layout structured around the following core sections:

*   **Home:** The primary landing page featuring search tools and latest updates.
*   **Post Advert:** Dedicated portal for job postings, corporate advertisements, and dynamic pricing tiers.
*   **Employers:** Tailored dashboard and resources for companies looking to hire talent.
*   **Help:** User documentation, frequently asked questions, and platform support.
*   **About Us:** Company background, vision, mission, core values, and central contact info.
*   **Contact Us:** Inbound communication channel for user inquiries.
*   **Testimonials:** User success stories from successfully placed candidates and satisfied employers.
